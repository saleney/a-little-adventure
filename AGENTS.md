# A Little Adventure Project Instructions

This project is an interactive choose-your-own-way story/adventure. Its purpose is to invite participation, imagination, movement, and a sense of discovery.

## Creative direction

- Preserve the warm, playful storybook quality.
- Keep choices understandable without making the experience feel over-explained.
- Let the visitor feel agency and curiosity rather than pressure to find a correct route.
- Avoid generic game UI if the existing storybook experience can do the job.

## Child-friendly experience

- Keep language and interactions appropriate for the intended young audience.
- Choices should be clear, emotionally safe, and easy to follow.
- Audio or visual elements should support the story rather than overwhelm it.
- Preserve open-ended imagination and gentle humor.

## UX and mobile

- Mobile is a first-class experience.
- Make story text readable and choice controls comfortably tappable.
- Prevent overflow, clipped text, and interactions that require precise mouse behavior.
- Keep progression and return/restart behavior clear.

## Code and scope

- `index.html` currently contains the entire project: structure, styling, story content, and interaction logic.
- Inspect before changing.
- Prefer the smallest clean implementation.
- Do not refactor the whole single-file site merely for architectural neatness unless explicitly requested.
- Preserve unrelated story branches and behavior.
- Keep agent work concise and token-efficient.

## Testing

For meaningful changes, walk through multiple branches and test on small mobile, standard mobile, large mobile, and desktop. Confirm that no path traps the visitor unintentionally.
